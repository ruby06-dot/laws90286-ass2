Northvale, a WTO Member, imposes a "Market Access Charge" of 12 per cent on electric scooters imported into Northvale. The obligation to pay the charge arises when the scooters are cleared through customs, and the amount is calculated on their customs value at the time of importation. To ease importers' cash flow, the charge is not paid at the border. Instead, the Northvale Domestic Revenue Agency collects it 60 days later at the importer's premises, together with the importer's other domestic taxes. Electric scooters made in Northvale are not subject to the charge or to any equivalent charge. Imported and domestic electric scooters are physically similar, are used for the same purposes, and fall under the same tariff heading. Northvale's bound tariff rate for electric scooters in its WTO Schedule is 5 per cent. Northvale describes the charge as an internal tax because it is collected inside the country.
