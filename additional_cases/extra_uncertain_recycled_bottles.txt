Lowland, a WTO Member, requires all bottled water sold in Lowland to be packaged in bottles made from at least 50 per cent recycled plastic. The rule applies equally to domestic and imported bottled water and does not mention origin. The rule takes effect in twelve months. Lowland has not published any information on how many domestic or foreign suppliers already meet the standard, or on the cost of switching to recycled plastic. Imported and domestic bottled water are sold side by side in the same shops at similar prices and are classified under the same tariff heading. Exporters in Eastport say that the rule will be much more expensive for them than for Lowland producers, but they have not provided any figures.
