Lowland, a WTO Member, requires all bottled water sold in Lowland to be packaged in bottles made from at least 50 per cent recycled plastic. The rule applies equally to domestic and imported bottled water and does not mention origin. Two of the three domestic producers already meet the standard; the third does not. Imported bottled water comes from several countries. Some foreign suppliers already meet the standard, while others would need to change their packaging. Lowland has not published any data on the cost of switching to recycled plastic. Imported and domestic bottled water are sold side by side in the same shops at similar prices and are classified under the same tariff heading. In the six months since the rule was announced, the market share of imported bottled water has stayed roughly the same.
